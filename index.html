Frequently asked questions

Can I hire you for a small project?

Yes. A focused analysis, dashboard, automation task or data audit can be scoped as a standalone project.

What if I don't know exactly what analysis I need?

That's fine. We can start with the business problem, available data and decision you are trying to make. The first step is to determine whether the data can answer the question and what approach makes sense.

Do you only work on Marketing Mix Modeling?

No. MMM is my current specialization, but my background also includes customer analytics, dashboards, automation, machine learning, NLP, time-series analysis and data science content.

Can you work with spreadsheets rather than a sophisticated data stack?

Yes. The right solution depends on the business. For some projects, Excel and Power BI are enough; for others, Python and SQL are more appropriate.

Can we start with a discovery call?

Yes. Send me a short description of the problem, what data you have and what outcome you want. We can then determine the most useful scope.

Have a data problem?

Tell me what you're trying to understand, improve or automate. If I can help, I'll suggest a practical way to scope it.
